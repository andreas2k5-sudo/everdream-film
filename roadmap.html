The Roadmap

A deliberate path from a world already on screen to a fully planned and finished production.

Production plan

Six stages. One destination.

The proof of concept is complete. Select a stage to see what it means for the production.

  1. 01
    Complete

    Concept and world-building

    Establish the story premise, characters, history, visual language, and the rules of Black Reef before production decisions are locked.

  2. 02
    Complete

    Proof of concept completed

    A 7–8 minute concept film now establishes the tone, world, central crew, and a practical foundation for the feature.

  3. 03
    Now

    Trailer and audience building

    Share the proof of concept, introduce the film to its first audience, and document the journey openly through Everdream Film and YouTube.

  4. 04
    Next

    Crowdfunding and support

    Open the public campaign, confirm supporter terms, and define the next production scope against the $8,000 funding goal.

  5. 05
    Ahead

    Feature development

    Refine the feature script, performances, visual references, sequence design, shot architecture, and workflow tests before full production.

  6. 06
    Ahead

    Production journey

    Move through planned production, editorial, sound, music, color, continuity, final polish, and the first supporter screening.

Built with care

The plan serves the film.

Each stage exists to protect story, character, and coherence as the production grows.

Specific dates will be published once campaign timing and the funded scope are confirmed. Progress updates will reflect real production milestones rather than artificial deadlines.
